I'm Pete — designer, nerd, dad, D&D-er. Online I mostly go by **twoshedzz**.

By background I'm a designer — a fine art degree first, then years at Capital Radio doing branding and website design, eventually running the design team there. From there I moved into the public sector, doing hands-on interaction design and plenty of time in the [GOV.UK prototyping kit](https://prototype-kit.service.gov.uk/docs/). These days I'm Head of Design at [Defra](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-environment-food-rural-affairs), the UK's environment department — a lot less hands-on building, a lot more of everything else.

I don't paint any more, but the fine art habit never really left: my approach to most things is still to throw something rough onto the canvas and gradually work it into a shape, rather than plan it out up front. Turns out that's a pretty good description of vibe-coding too.

Missing that hands-on making is honestly what kicked off this whole blog: an itch to start learning to code again. I gave up on that pretty quickly in the traditional sense — courses, tutorials, that kind of thing — and ended up vibe-coding with AI instead, directing rather than typing. It's become more of a running diary since — a place to note down where I am on that slow, messy, occasionally uncomfortable journey, mostly so future-me has something honest to look back on. Typos and all. If you want the whole arc, the [archive](/archives/) is chronological.

Outside of that, I've got two long-running obsessions:

- **Blood Bowl.** A tabletop game of miniature fantasy football played by orcs, elves, and worse. I collect (and very slowly paint) far more teams than any reasonable person should — a proper guilty pleasure, tracked on [The Pile of Shame](/posts/the-pile-of-shame/).
- **Dungeons & Dragons**, on and off for over 40 years. These days I run a small campaign for my son and his friends — [The Ruins of Ethium](https://ruinsofethium.netlify.app/) — and I've started using AI to help write it up between sessions.
